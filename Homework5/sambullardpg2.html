Deltarune is a currently ongoing indie jrpg videogame that acts as a psuedo sequel to the game Undertale. It tells the story of two teenagers, Kris and Susie, who stumble across a magical otherworld called the dark world, where ordinary innanimate objects are given humanoid forms and take the roles of kings, dukes, and other fantasy titles. There they meet the Prince of the Dark Ralsei, and are informed that they are heroes of an ancient proc=phecy to seal the dark fountains and stop the Roaring Knight. The game has released episodically in "chapters", with 5 chapters our of a planned 7 (possible 8 but i don't have time to get into that right now) currently released as of writing this.
